2. What is the precise arrival date used to calculate the 30-day registration window?

**Key facts:**
- Policy number: PSHC-2026-M-88421; Reference: PSHC-RN-2026-44128
- Current cover: 1 Sep 2026 – 31 Aug 2027; Renewal effective: 1 Sep 2027
- Premium due: CNY 1,280 by 15 August 2027

**Watch-outs (severity):**

**3 questions the document does NOT answer:**
