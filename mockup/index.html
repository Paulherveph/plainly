Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Plainly explains what a document says and points to the exact lines. For decisions with legal weight, talk to a professional.

Which documents work?

PDFs, photos (PNG, JPG) and pasted text, up to 10 MB and 20 pages. Leases, visa and residence letters, job offers, bank notices and contracts work best.

What if my document is in another language?

Upload it as it is. You choose whether the explanation comes back in English, French or Chinese.

How do I know it didn't make things up?

Each point carries a quote. Plainly checks that the quote really is in your document and removes any point that fails the check.

Do I need an account?

No. You can try it right away with 5 analyses a day. Sign in with Google or an email link to keep your documents and get 20 a day.

Privacy, in plain language

You trust Plainly with documents that matter. Here is exactly what happens to them.

What we store

  • The file you upload, in a private storage bucket only your account can read.
  • The analysis and your chat messages about that document.
  • A daily count of analyses, to apply the free limit.

How long

30 days. After that, the file, its analysis and its chat are deleted automatically. You can delete everything sooner with the button below.

Who sees it

Only you. The text is sent to the AI model (Anthropic Claude) to produce the explanation, and is not used to train it. Document text is never written to logs or analytics.

Not legal advice

Plainly explains what a document says. It does not tell you whether something is legal or what you should sign.

Delete everything now

Removes all your documents, analyses, chats and deadlines. This can't be undone.
