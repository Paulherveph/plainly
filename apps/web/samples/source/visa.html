Notice: residence permit renewal

File no. 2026/RP/48213. Holder: Wei Zhang.

Your current residence permit expires on 15 January 2027.

To renew it, you must book an appointment online and submit your application no later than 15 December 2026.

Applications submitted after this date may not be processed before the expiry of your permit.

A stamp duty of €225 is payable online when you submit your application.

Documents required

Bring the original and one copy of: your passport, your current permit, three recent photographs and proof of address dated less than three months.

Students must also provide a certificate of enrolment for the current academic year.

Incomplete files will be returned without examination.

If you change address, you must notify the prefecture within three months.
