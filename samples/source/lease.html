Residential Lease Agreement

This agreement is made between Marc Petit (the Landlord) and Amina Diallo (the Tenant) for the apartment at 14 Rue des Lilas, Apt 3B, 69003 Lyon.

§1 Term. The lease begins on 1 December 2026 and runs for a period of twelve (12) months.

§2 Rent. The monthly rent is €850, payable on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.

§3 Charges. A fixed monthly charge of €60 covers building maintenance and cold water.

§4 Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of €1,700 no later than 20 November 2026.

§5 Signature. The Tenant must sign and return this agreement by 5 November 2026.

Obligations of the Tenant

§6 Insurance. The Tenant must provide a certificate of home insurance before 1 December 2026.

§7 Late payment. Any rent unpaid more than five days after the due date incurs a late fee of 10% of the monthly rent.

§8 Repairs. Minor repairs costing less than €150 are the responsibility of the Tenant.

§9 Pets. No animals may be kept in the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

§10 Access. The Landlord may enter the premises with at least 24 hours' notice for inspection or repairs.

Renewal and termination

§11 Renewal. This lease renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives notice.

§12 Notice. The Tenant must give three months' written notice by registered letter to end the lease.

§13 Joint liability. Where several tenants sign, they are jointly and severally liable for the rent.

Signed in Lyon, in two copies.
